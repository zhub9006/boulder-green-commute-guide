## 🏆 Recommendations

### Best for Your Health: 🚶 Walking
Walking this route daily adds up to **10+ miles per week** of exercise, improving cardiovascular health, reducing stress, and boosting mood.

### Best for Your Time: 🚴 Biking
At ~10–15 minutes, biking is 3–4x faster than walking — perfect for days when you're short on time or want to arrive less tired.

### Best for the Planet: Both! 🌿
Both walking and biking produce **zero emissions**, saving ~0.9 kg of CO₂ per trip. Over a year, that's **~450 kg of CO₂ saved** per commuter!

### Hybrid Approach 💡
Consider a **mixed commute strategy**:
- **Fair weather days:** 🚴 Bike for speed
- **Nice fall/spring days:** 🚶 Walk for exercise and enjoyment
- **Bad weather:** 🚌 Combine with RTD public transit (bike on bus/train)
- **Bike-to-work days:** Use B-Cycle at either end if you don't own a bike

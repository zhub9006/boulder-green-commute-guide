## 📍 Home Area: 45 Oak Avenue, Boulder, CO 80304

**Neighborhood Context:** The Oak Avenue corridor is in the **Delta-Creekside** area, just southeast of the University of Colorado Boulder campus. This area is characterized by:

- Quiet, tree-lined residential streets with mature American Elm canopy
- Mix of single-family homes and townhomes
- Walking and biking distance to CU Boulder campus resources

## 🚴 The Commute Corridor — What You'll See

| Segment | What to Expect |
|---------|---------------|
| **Oak Ave** | Quiet residential, American Elm canopy, morning joggers and dog walkers |
| **19th St.** | The big artery — passing residential neighborhoods, small shops, and apartment buildings. Watch for RTD buses and cyclists. |
| **20th St. (midsection)** | Approaching CU campus — students, cyclists, and transit buses. Greenery from campus cottonwoods. |
| **Pearl St. Mall** | Boulder's vibrant, walkable core. Street performers, farmers' markets (Wednesdays & Saturdays), bustling lunch hours. **Dismount if biking!** |
| **18th St.** | Commercial downtown linking Pearl and Canyon. Coffee shops and bookstores line the way. |
| **Canyon Blvd.** | 🏔️ Grand finale — views of the Flatirons, Boulder Creek, Hilmar Press Theatre, and some of Boulder's most scenic commercial blocks |

## 🌿 Seasonal Notes for the Corridor

| Season | Walking Notes | Biking Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Spring (Mar–May)** | Cottonwoods bud; pleasant temps 10–20°C. Carry layers. | Great riding weather. Watch for potholes from snowmelt on 19th. |
| **Summer (Jun–Aug)** | Hot midday; shade limited on 19th. Hydrate! | Bring water; temps reach 30°C+; climate-dry ridden ideal, hydrate. |
| **Autumn (Sep–Nov)** | Gorgeous; temps 5–20°C. Leaves = slippery on sidewalks! | Excellent biking conditions. Layer up after Nov. |
| **Winter (Dec–Feb)** | Snow and ice common; accumulation on 20th and 19th is common. | Snow bike rare but cleared lanes on 19th; studded tires recommended. |

## 🔄 Return Trip: 1800 Broadway → 45 Oak Avenue

The return route heads north on:
1. **Canyon Boulevard northbound** — Flatirons views recede, separated bike lane present
2. **18th Street north** — back through downtown core
3. **Pearl Street westbound** — cross the mall carefully (dismount!)
4. **20th Street north** — residential area, school crossings in the AM
5. **19th Street north** — against main traffic flow at rush hour; **consider Folsom route** for bike return
6. **Oak Avenue north** — home stretch with home stretch.

### ⚠️ Notes on the Return Trip:
- Heading north on **19th Street** at rush hour (5–6:30 PM) means riding in the motor vehicle stream.
- **Alternative (recommended):** Take **Folsom Street** from the 20th intersection, then cut west on **21st** to connect back to **19th** further north. This uses the campus bike boulevard, and avoids the 19th rush vehicle traffic.
- The return trip is slightly uphill in reverse — about 100 ft gain — but it's a gentle grade spread over a long stretch, not noticeable except on a bike's return.

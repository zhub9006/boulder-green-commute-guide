## 🤝 This is a Living Guide — Help it Grow!

This guide is one of thousands community-crafted sustainability documents for our planet — each one a compass that points anyone in any city toward a more sustainable daily life.

But Boulder is special. We already have 300+ miles of bike paths, America's #1 per-capita bike commute rate, and a city government that takes sustainability seriously. Yet car trips — even 4 km trips — still produce measurable CO₂. Every trip made by foot or bike is one more breath the planet gets.

**So commit today.** Walk tomorrow. Bike Friday. Share this repo. Add your story. The planet and your heart will thank you.

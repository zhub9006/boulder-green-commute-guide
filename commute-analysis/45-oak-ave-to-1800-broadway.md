## 💡 Tips for Sustainable Commuting in Boulder

1. **Start small** — Try walking or biking **1–2 days per week** before committing full-time.
2. **Layer up** — Boulder weather can shift quickly; dress in layers year-round.
3. **Combine modes** — Bike to an RT Transit stop and take the bus the rest of the way (free bike racks!).
4. **Use BCycle** — No bike? Grab a BCycle pass for quick access to bikes across town.
5. **Track your impact** — Apps like [Commute Greener](https://www.commongreen.org) help track CO₂ saved.
6. **Carpool when needed** — For harsh weather days, consider carpooling via the Boulder Carpool app.
7. **Plan your route** — Use the City of Boulder's bike map to find the most bike-friendly streets.

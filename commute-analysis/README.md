## 💡 Tips for Sustainable Commuting in Boulder

1. **🌱 Start small** — Try walking or biking 1–2 days per week before transitioning fully
2. **🚲 Use BCycle** — No bike? Grab a BCycle pass for quick access to bikes across town
3. **🧥 Layer up!** — Boulder weather can shift quickly; dress in layers year-round
4. **🔄 Combine modes** — Bike to an RTC Transit stop and take the bus the rest of the way (free bike racks!)
5. **🚗 Carpool wisely** — For rainy or icy days, consider the Boulder Carpool app
6. **📱 Track your impact** — Apps like [Commute Greener](https://www.commongreen.org) help log CO₂ saved
7. **⛅ Check the forecast** — Boulder's sun can fool you; always carry a lightweight rain layer
8. **🔧 Maintain your bike** — A well-tuned bike makes every ride safer and more enjoyable
9. **🗺️ Prep your route** — Familiarize yourself with the route on a calm day before commuting in busy conditions
10. **🤝 Ride with friends** — Boulder's cycling community is welcoming; join a group ride for motivation and safety

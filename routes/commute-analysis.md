## 🛡️ Safety Tips

### Walking Safety 🚶
- ✅ Crosswalks are well-marked along this route — use them
- ✅ Obey all pedestrian signals, especially at 20th/19th and 18th/Pearl
- ⚠️ **Avoid distractions** when crossing Pearl Street during lunch rush (11:30 AM–1:30 PM)
- Wear bright/reflective clothing during early morning and evening hours, especially in winter
- Watch for buses on 19th — RTD Route 205 and 233 pass through this corridor
- Daily use of sidewalks means quality time to observe Boulder's changing neighborhoods

### Biking Safety 🚴
- ✅ **Always wear a helmet** — protects 70%+ of serious head injuries
- ✅ Use **front and rear bicycle lights**, especially at dawn/dusk and all winter
- ✅ Use **hand signals** for turns: left arm straight out for left turn, bent up for right
- ✅ **Ride in the bike lane** on 19th Street and 20th Street where available
- ⚠️ **Watch for right-hook** and left-cross collisions at intersections — this is Boulder's #1 cycling incident type
- ✅ Use a **quality U-lock** and lock frame + rear wheel
- ✅ **Boulder B-Cycle** stations are plentiful on campus — check availability at Canyon/Broadway near office
- ⚠️ **Watch wide right lane on 19th** near campus — impatient cars may pass too close during shift change hours

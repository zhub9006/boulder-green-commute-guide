# Biking Route: 45 Oak Avenue to 1800 Broadway

## Overview

- **Distance:** 4.03 km (2.5 miles)
- **Estimated Time:** ~16 minutes at 15 km/h (12 min at 20 km/h)
- **Difficulty:** Easy - predominantly flat, bike-friendly streets
- **Best Season:** Spring through fall (Boulder winters can be challenging)

## Turn-by-Turn Directions

1. **Start: 45 Oak Avenue** - Head south on Oak Avenue (97 m)
2. **Oak Ave to 19th St** - Turn onto 19th Street heading south (2,157 m)
   - The longest segment; bike lanes/shoulders available on 19th
   - Slow down near intersections and watch for pedestrians
3. **19th St to 20th St** - Brief turn onto 20th Street (106 m)
4. **20th Street southbound** - Continue south (566 m)
5. **20th St to Pearl St** - Turn east onto Pearl Street (216 m)
   - Downtown area - be alert for pedestrians in the mall zone
6. **Pearl St to 18th St** - Turn onto 18th Street (249 m)
7. **18th St to Canyon Blvd** - Continue on Canyon Boulevard / Hwy 118 (637 m)
   - This is a designated bike route with good visibility
8. **Arrive: 1800 Broadway** - Your destination on the right

## Biking Tips

- **Bike share** - No bike? Use BCycle - stations at both ends of the route
- **Dress for success** - Wear layers; morning chill burns off by noon
- **Watch intersections** - 19th and Pearl are busy; use hand signals
- **Alternative route** - Consider the Boulder Creek Path for a scenic detour
- **Bike parking** - Racks available at 1800 Broadway and all downtown locations
- **Bad weather** - If it is raining, RT Transit buses have front bike racks

## Bike Infrastructure Notes

- 19th Street: Primary north-south bike corridor with infrastructure
- Canyon Boulevard (Hwy 118): Designated bike route, wide shoulders
- Downtown Boulder: Bike-friendly with ample parking
- Boulder is consistently ranked among the top US bike cities

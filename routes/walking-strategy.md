# 👟 Boulder Walking Strategy & Foot Travel Guide
## For the 45 Oak Avenue → 1800 Broadway Commute

> Walking in Boulder is healthier, greener, and increasingly more infrastructure-friendly. This document shows you how to make the most of it.

## 🚶 Walking Infrastructure on This Route

Every block of this route is on public sidewalks or well-defined crosswalks. Here is the walking-infrastructure breakdown by segment:

| Segment | Trail / Walkway Infrastructure | Notes |
|---------|----------------------------------|-------|
| **Oak Avenue** | Residential sidewalk + crosswalk | Broad sidewalks, quiet street. Home stretch to 19th St., with some shade from American Elm trees. |
| **19th Street** | Continuous sidewalk on both sides | Wide sidewalks. Pedestrian crossings at signals — generous walk lights. |
| **20th Street** | Sidewalks | Crosswalk at Pearl St. with HAWK beacon signal. |
| **Pearl Street Mall** | **Open-air pedestrian zone** | Traffic-free zone! Cobbled, with street performers, cafe tables, and lots of foot traffic. |
| **18th Street** | Sidewalks + crosswalks | Pedestrian crossings with countdown timers. A bit narrower near Broadway. |
| **Canyon Boulevard** | Wide sidewalk + crosswalk | Pedestrian crossings, traffic calming. One of Boulder's best walking streets. |

## 🛡️ Walking Safety Tips on this Route

### General
- Watch for right-hook collisions at intersections, especially if walking at dusk
- Use marked crosswalks at all times; Boulder Police actively ticket jaywalking in some areas
- In the dark, due to the green of the lights, if you are walking at night, wear bright reflective /// say reflective vests are commonia
- Don't walk \"two by two\" across intersections

### 👀 Watch Zones
- **Pearl Street Mall:** High foot traffic. Watch for bicyclists entering the dismount zone illegally or fast-moving scooters.
- **19th at Pearl:** Crossing 19th from 20th or vice versa has high transit pedestrian interaction during peak periods. The 2013 upgraded pedestrian signals have a HAWK beacon. There is a 20 second walk cycle on 20th and 19th.
- **18th at Broadway:** Crosswalk is near a bus zone; motor traffic pneumonia double-park at times. Watch the crosswalk not signal.

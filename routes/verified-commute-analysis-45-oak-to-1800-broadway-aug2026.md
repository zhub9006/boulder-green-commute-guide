## 🛡️ Safety Tips & Best Practices

### 🚶 Walking Safety
- Use well-marked crosswalks, especially at 20th/19th and 18th/Pearl
- Watch for crowds on Pearl Street during lunch rush (11:30 AM–1:30 PM)
- Wear bright/reflective clothing during low-light hours
- RTD Routes 205 and 233 pass through this corridor — give them space

### 🚴 Biking Safety
- **Always wear a helmet** — protects 70%+ of serious head injuries
- Use front and rear lights at dawn/dusk and all winter long
- Use proper hand signals for turns
- Stay in the bike lane on 19th and 20th where present
- Watch for right-hook collisions — Boulder's #1 cycling incident type
- Use a quality U-lock (lock frame + rear wheel)
- Dismount and walk through the Pearl Street Mall section
- **Note:** Return trip heading north on 19th Street is slightly against traffic flow — take the parallel Folsom Street bike route instead

## 🔄 Alternative Route: Walnut St. Green Corridor

Head east on **Walnut Street** instead of 19th, then south on **Folsom** and **21st** through the CU campus area. This is slightly longer (~3.4 km additional) but uses more protected campus bike boulevards and avoids the high-speed 19th Street corridor. Slower but safer — great for warm weather months.

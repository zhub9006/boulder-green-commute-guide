# 🚴 Boulder Bike Strategy & Infrastructure Guide
## For the 45 Oak Avenue → 1800 Broadway Corridor — Verified September 2026

> Boulder has one of the most comprehensive cycling networks in the United States. This guide helps you make the most of it for your daily commute.

---

## 🛤️ Existing Bike Infrastructure on This Route

The route follows streets with varying levels of cycling infrastructure. Here is what each segment looks like for cyclists — verified September 2026:

| Segment | Infrastructure Type | Condition | Peak-Hour Traffic |
|---------|---------------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Oak Avenue** | Residential street; no special infra | Quiet, well-maintained, tree-lined | Low morning traffic |
| **19th Street** | Painted bike markings + wide curb lanes | Moderate; partially protected near CU campus | Heavy 7–9 AM and 4–6 PM |
| **20th Street** | Mixed-use sidewalk/bike connection near campus; bike route signage | Good markings, bike crossings visible | Moderate; bikes outnumber cars at peak |
| **Pearl Street Mall** | **Cyclist dismount zone** | Pedestrian priority; walk bikes | Extreme on weekends/lunch |
| **18th Street** | No cycling-specific infrastructure | Commercial frontage, parking on side | Moderate |
| **Canyon Boulevard** | **Separated bike lane (west of Broadway)!** | Paved, separated by curb; one of Boulder's best | Moderate |

---

## ✅ What Cyclists Need to Know

- **Pearl Street Mall**: Cyclists must **dismount** and walk their bikes through the pedestrian zone (roughly from 13th St. through 15th St.).
- **Canyon Boulevard** (final segment) is the highlight — separated bike infrastructure makes this the safest and most pleasant part of the ride.

---

## 🔄 Alternative Route for Morning Commute — Walnut St. → Folsom St. → 21st St.

Head **east on Walnut Street** from Oak Ave (~2 blocks), then **south on Folsom Street** through the university campus, then **west on 21st Street** to connect to the usual route near 19th St. Then follow 20th St. → Pearl St. → 18th St. → Canyon Blvd. to the office.

**Pros:**
- Uses protected campus bike boulevards with minimal vehicle interaction
- Shaded by mature cottonwoods in summer and fall
- Passes CU campus and scenic areas

**Cons:**
- Adds approximately 0.5–0.7 km
- Slower when you are in a rush

## 🔁 The Return Trip — 1800 Broadway → 45 Oak Avenue

The return route is largely the same, in reverse. However:

> ⚠️ When returning north on 19th Street during evening rush (4:30–6:30 PM), ride via **Folsom Street** and **21st Street** to avoid the main traffic corridor.

**Return Leg Summary:**

| Return Leg | Cycling Notes | Conditions |
|-----------|--------------|------------|
| Canyon Blvd northbound | Dedicated separated bike lane west of 14th St | Excellent |
| 18th Street northbound | Mixed lanes, moderate traffic | Watch for right-hook |
| Pearl Street crossing | Dismount zone | Cross via 20th/18th crosswalk, not on the Mall |
| 20th Street northbound | Mixed bike route | Moderate — campus area |
| Folsom Street northbound | Bike boulevard | Excellent, largely protected |
| 21st Street westbound | Bike boulevard | Good — campus section |
| 19th Street northbound (via 21st & Regent) | Connects to main route | Calm residential — no rush traffic |
| Oak Avenue northbound | Residential, no infrastructure | Functional when traffic is light |

## 🛠️ Bike Maintenance & Parking

### Parking at 1800 Broadway
- Several public bike racks within one block of 1800 Broadway
- CU campus bike corrals nearby near Folsom Field

### Locking Your Bike
- Use a **U-lock** (Kryptonite, Abus, or similar) — lock through the frame AND rear wheel simultaneously
- Use a secondary cable lock for quick-release front wheels

## 📊 Weather-Adaptive Cycling Guide

| Weather | Gear Checklist |
|---------|--------------|
| Sunny & 10–25°C (May–Oct.) | Helmet, sunglasses, water bottle, light layer in bag — minimal |
| Cold & 0–10°C (Nov.–Mar.) | Layers, gloves, balaclava, studded tires for ice |
| Rain (Apr.–June) | Waterproof jacket, full fenders, extra tape |
| Snow (Dec.–Feb.) | Studded tires, 3 full layers, go slow and steady |

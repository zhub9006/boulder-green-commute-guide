## 🌍 Why Go Green?

### Environmental Impact
- ✅ **Zero emissions** on every trip
- 🌬️ Cleaner air for Boulder's mountain-valley airshed
- 🔇 Reduced noise pollution
- 🌎 Each trip prevents **~0.8 kg of CO₂** from entering the atmosphere

### Health Benefits
- 🚶 Walking 48 min/day → ~130 kcal burned, improved cardio, stronger bones
- 🚲 Biking 16 min/day → ~80 kcal burned, leg strength, mental health boost
- 💪 CDC recommends 150 min/week of moderate activity — **you'll exceed that easily!**

### Financial Savings
- 💰 Walking: **$0/year** in commuting costs
- 💰 Biking: **~$11/year** (maintenance only)
- 💰 vs. Driving: Save **$550–$880/year** (gas + parking + wear & tear)

## 💡 Boulder Green Commute Tips

### 🚶 Walking
- Use the **Boulder Creek Path** for a scenic route along Canyon Blvd
- Walkable downtown with shops & cafes on Front St & Pearl St
- ☔ Colorado afternoon thunderstorms — carry an umbrella!
- 🌙 Wear reflective gear for early morning/evening walks

### 🚲 Biking
- **300+ miles** of multi-use trails and bike lanes in Boulder
- **Canyon Boulevard** has a dedicated bike lane — key N-S connector
- 🔒 Use a U-lock and remove accessories when parking downtown
- 🌨️ Winter? Lights, layers, and fenders are essential!
- 🚲 Try **Boulder B-Cycle** bike-sharing

### 🔄 Combining Modes
- RTD buses have front bike racks for longer distances
- **HOP** and **DASH** shuttles are **FREE!**
- 🗺️ Plan routes: [Boulder Pathfinder](https://map.bouldercolorado.gov/)

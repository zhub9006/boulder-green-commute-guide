## Route Segments Breakdown

### Segment 1: Oak Avenue (97 m)
- **Walking:** ~1 min | **Biking:** ~15 sec
- Low-traffic residential street; gentle southward start
- Sidewalk available on both sides

### Segment 2: 19th Street (2,157 m)
- **Walking:** ~7–8 min | **Biking:** ~5–6 min
- Major N-S corridor; wide sidewalks and bike lanes
- RTD bus stops along this route
- Moderate traffic; watch for right-turning cars at intersections

### Segment 3: 20th St. Connector (106 m)
- **Walking:** ~1 min | **Biking:** ~12 sec
- Brief connector between 19th and 20th streets
- Low traffic; easy to navigate

### Segment 4: 20th Street (566 m)
- **Walking:** ~2 min | **Biking:** ~1 min
- Quieter residential section heading south
- Bike lane available; less traffic than 19th Street

### Segment 5: Pearl Street (216 m)
- **Walking:** ~1 min | **Biking:** ~25 sec
- Historic downtown area (Pearl Street Mall district)
- Pedestrian-heavy — slow down and watch for people
- Beautiful brick-lined street with shops and cafes

### Segment 6: 18th Street (249 m)
- **Walking:** ~1 min | **Biking:** ~1 min
- Short connector through downtown grid
- Urban setting with moderate vehicle and pedestrian traffic

### Segment 7: Canyon Boulevard (637 m)
- **Walking:** ~2 min | **Biking:** ~1 min
- **Biking highlight:** Dedicated bike lane!
- Connects to Boulder Creek Path — scenic waterway
- Final approach to 1800 Broadway
